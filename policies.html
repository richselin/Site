Studio Policies

Appointments

To provide the best experience for every client, appointments are reserved specifically for you and your treatment time.

Please arrive on time for your appointment to allow for a relaxed and unhurried experience.

Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may require a shortened service to preserve scheduled appointments.

Cancellations & Missed Appointments

Please provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling whenever possible.

Repeated missed appointments or excessive late cancellations may limit future booking availability.

Refunds

All services are non-refundable.

If you experience concerns regarding your treatment or skin response, please contact Sonya Dilbeck Esthetics within 48 hours so we may appropriately support you.

Retail Products

Unopened retail products may be exchanged or returned within 14 days of purchase.

Due to the nature of professional skincare products, opened or used products cannot be returned.

Minors

Clients under 18 require parent or guardian consent, and a parent or guardian must be present at the initial appointment.

Health & Safety

Please notify Sonya Dilbeck Esthetics prior to your appointment if you are pregnant, nursing, experiencing illness, using prescription skincare products, or have recently undergone cosmetic or medical procedures.

Your safety, comfort, and skin health are always the priority.

Photography

Before-and-after photographs and/or videos may be taken for treatment documentation, progress tracking, and educational purposes.

Client privacy and comfort are always respected. Separate written consent is required before any photographs or videos are used for marketing, website, social media, or promotional purposes.

Results Disclaimer

Skincare results vary based on individual skin condition, lifestyle, consistency, home care, and overall health. While every treatment is customized to support your skin goals, specific results cannot be guaranteed.
